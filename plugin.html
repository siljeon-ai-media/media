Hide the date on every "Notice" post. In one rule.

Hide Everything lets you hide a post's title, featured image, author, date or category. Pro adds the parts that save time on sites with many posts.

Get Pro (from $39, one-time)Free version on WordPress.org

What Pro adds

  • Rules by category, tag, content type, author role or template — e.g. "posts in Notices: hide the date".
  • Bulk apply a Hide/Show choice to existing posts in batches, with one-step undo.
  • Import / export your settings and rules as one JSON file (license key and per-post choices are never exported).
Good to know
  • Needs the free Hide Everything plugin active.
  • A post's own Hide/Show choice always wins over a rule.
  • Tested so far on WordPress 7.1 / PHP 8.3 with a block theme. Classic themes, multisite and older PHP/WordPress versions are not tested yet — if something breaks, write to us and we will fix it.
